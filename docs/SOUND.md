# Sound

TruFocus ships its own sound so the moments of a flow feel like something: a chime when a task
is crossed off, an airy swell into a micro-rest, a lift back into focus, a gong when the day is
shut down. All of it is optional (Settings ▸ Sound) and plays only on the device you acted on.

## Cues

| Moment | File | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task done | `tf_complete.m4a` | CB Sounddesign — *Activation 2*, “Feedback 36 up” |
| Switch task (juggling) | `tf_switch.m4a` | CB Sounddesign — *Activation 2*, “UI Zooms 103” |
| Back to it / rest over | `tf_go.m4a` | Rescopic Sound — *Distinct Whooshes*, “Pure Sci-Fi Whoosh Fast 03” |
| Rest begins | `tf_rest.m4a` | Rescopic Sound — *Distinct Whooshes*, “Airy Whoosh Wind Gust 11” |
| Stuck → think | `tf_think.m4a` | Rescopic Sound — *User Interaction*, “Window Open Thin 05” |
| Booted out | `tf_boot.m4a` | Doex Studio — *Qantum UI*, “Noisy Impact 09” |
| Day shut down | `tf_shutdown.m4a` | Orbital Emitter — *Cinematic Transitions Vol. 2*, “The Gong” |
| Under rests | `tf_rest_ambience.m4a` | Stefano Cremona — *Rivers, Streams, Creeks*, “Calm River 02” (60 s seamless loop) |

All from the Sonniss GDC Game Audio Bundle, 96 kHz / 24-bit masters. Licence: royalty-free for
commercial use in apps and games, no attribution required, not to be redistributed as sound
effects (https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc). Trimmed, loudness-matched (−18 LUFS, −2 dBTP) and
encoded to AAC 256 kbps.

## Focus music

| Choice | File | Style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drive | `tf_music_drive.m4a` | Progressive house, 128 BPM |
| Pulse | `tf_music_pulse.m4a` | Liquid drum & bass, 172 BPM |
| Flow | `tf_music_flow.m4a` | Lo-fi house, 112 BPM |

Original instrumentals made for TruFocus with ElevenLabs Music (v2.5). Each was picked from four
takes for steady energy (no breakdowns), cut to a whole number of bars on the beat grid with a
crossfade so the ~171 s loop has no seam, and mastered to −16 LUFS / AAC 192 kbps.

**Apple Music** (iPhone): for subscribers, plays Apple Music's “Pure Focus” playlist. Without a
subscription (or before the MusicKit service is enabled on the App ID at developer.apple.com ▸
Identifiers ▸ TruFocus (`com.youngjingoh.truefocus`) ▸ App Services ▸ MusicKit) it falls back to Drive.

Music keeps playing with the iPhone locked (background audio) and mixes with other apps. It
ducks to a third while you think and fades out for rests, where the river takes over.
