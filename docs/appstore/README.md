## How they are presented

Modelled on how Opal presents its listing:

- **A light field.** Warm brand Paper with soft peach and white washes that drift from frame to frame, so the eight read as one panorama.
- **One straight-on phone per frame,** about three quarters of the width, starting under the headline and fading into fog at the bottom instead of ending on a hard edge.
- **A centred two-weight headline** in brand Ink: regular for the setup, bold for the payoff.
- **One element lifted out of the screen** and enlarged past the phone's edge (the blocked feeds, the nudge, the Live Activity, the Reply pass).
- **Distractions drift at the frame edges** (badges, Reels tiles, hearts, chat bubbles), soft-focused and never reaching the phone: the ring keeps everything out.
- **Proof on the first frame** ("Built on 8 published studies") and the flat brand lockup on the last, as the brand kit asks for on light backgrounds.

Every screen is drawn from the app's SwiftUI views and copy (shield, nudge and menu bar wording are the app's defaults in the Direct tone); names and tasks are sample content. On iPhone the Reply pass ends on time, since iOS keeps apps from seeing scrolling inside other apps. Scroll-based boot-outs happen in browsers and Mac feed apps.
