The eight frames are one panorama: the ember orbit line runs from one screenshot into the next as people swipe. Every screen is drawn from the app's SwiftUI views and copy (the shield, nudge and menu bar wording are the app's defaults in the Direct tone); the names and tasks are sample content.

On iPhone the Reply pass ends on time, since iOS keeps apps from seeing scrolling inside other apps. Scroll-based boot-outs happen in browsers and Mac feed apps.
