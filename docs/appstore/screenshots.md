# Screenshot shot list

Eight iPhone shots and seven Mac shots. Each one lists the screen state, a short caption headline
(with an optional subline), and how to stage it. The order is the order on the store page. The first
three shots carry the pitch, because most people never swipe further.

## Rules for every shot

- **Sizes.** iPhone 6.9": **1320 × 2868** portrait (iPhone 16/17 Pro Max). App Store Connect scales
  it down for smaller iPhones. Mac: **2880 × 1800** (16:10). 2560 × 1600, 1440 × 900 and 1280 × 800
  are also accepted.
- **Tone set to Direct** (Settings ▸ Voice) before you capture. **No profanity anywhere**: guideline
  2.3.8 requires screenshots suitable for 4+ even if the app is rated 16+/17+.
- **No third-party logos, app icons or feed content.** Don't show the Instagram, TikTok or YouTube
  UI (guideline 5.2.1). Where a URL bar would show a brand's domain, crop it out or use the generic
  boot-out page.
- **No adult domain names** in any shot.
- **Demo data, no personal data.** Use this one plan everywhere:
  - The win: **"Investor update out by noon"**
  - Tasks: **Investor update** (current), **Reply to Sam about the term sheet** (juggled, waiting),
    **Fix onboarding bug**, **Record the demo**, **Ship pricing page** (done), **Book flights**
    (done)
  - Breadcrumb on "Investor update": *"Draft numbers done — write the ask"*
- **Clock at 9:41**, full battery, full signal. On a device, either schedule the capture at 9:41 or
  composite the status bar. Screen Time shields and the Live Activity only work on a real device, so
  the Simulator's `simctl status_bar override` isn't available for those shots.
- **Caption style** (from `docs/DESIGN.md`): the headline in SF Pro Display Bold on the night canvas
  `#050506`, an Ember `#FF5A1F` dot before it, the subline in `ink2`. Keep the headline to six words or
  fewer. The device frame is optional, but keep it consistent across the whole set.
- Pick **one appearance** (dark suits the brand, since the focus canvas is always night) and use it
  for every shot.
