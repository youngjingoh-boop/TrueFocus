Common questions

How does blocking work?

One rule on every device. On a task, the feeds you picked (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, X) are closed on your iPhone, Mac and browser, Reels and Shorts too. Messages stay open. Off a task (paused, resting or stopped) everything opens again, with a reminder after the time you set.

I need to reply to a message during a task

On iPhone, tap Message on the shield: the app opens for a few minutes, then closes again. On the web, DMs and messaging stay open during tasks.

I use Instagram or Facebook for work

Turn on for work for that platform (in Settings or the browser extension). It stays open during tasks with its feed hidden, and after two minutes of scrolling TruFocus asks: working or scrolling?

An app I need is blocked

On iPhone, add it under Guard ▸ Always allowed. If you picked a whole category under Scroll apps, every app in it is blocked during tasks, and Always allowed lets the ones you need through.

I see a grey "Restricted" screen

If it appears during a task, add the app under Guard ▸ Always allowed, and in the Scroll apps picker make sure no website the app relies on (such as facebook.com for Business Suite) is ticked. Outside a task it comes from elsewhere: check Settings ▸ Screen Time ▸ Content & Privacy Restrictions, and turn off any blocks in other Screen Time apps before deleting them.

Syncing my iPhone and Mac

Sync is off until you turn it on in Settings ▸ Sync. To link a device, enter the six-digit code from the other one and check both screens show the same four-digit number. Everything is end-to-end encrypted.

Deleting my data

Deleting the app removes everything on that device. To remove the encrypted copy on our server, use Settings ▸ Sync ▸ Delete server copy…. See the privacy policy for details.
