TruFocus Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026. TruFocus is made by Youngjin Koh ("we"). This policy covers the TruFocus apps for iPhone and Mac and the TruFocus browser extension for Safari and Chrome.

The short version

  • There's no account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • Your Screen Time choices, your app usage and your browsing stay on your device.
  • Sync between your iPhone and Mac is off until you turn it on. When it's on, your tasks, notes and focus timeline are end-to-end encrypted: sealed on your device with a key that stays on your devices, so we can't read them.
  • You can turn sync off, or delete everything on our server, in Settings ▸ Sync.

What stays on your device

  • Screen Time. With your permission, TruFocus uses Apple's Screen Time framework to block the apps you pick during tasks, remind you (or lock the apps, if you choose) after time in them off a task, and filter adult websites. Apple gives TruFocus only anonymous tokens for the apps and sites you choose, not their names or your usage history. These stay on your iPhone and are never uploaded.
  • Browsing. The browser extension checks the address of pages on a short list of feed sites (such as Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and LinkedIn) and how long and how far you scroll there, so it can close feeds and Reels during tasks and remind you off them. It blocks known adult sites. It decides all of this on your device. It doesn't read page text, forms or cookies, and it never sends the pages you visit to us or anyone else. While a focus task is running, it tells the TruFocus app on the same device which site the active tab is on and the tab's title (not the full address), for the drift guard below. On a Mac, it talks only to the TruFocus app on the same Mac.
  • Feed apps on the Mac. While a feed app is in front, the Mac app counts the time and how far you scroll. It never reads what's on screen or what you type.
  • The drift guard on the Mac. While a focus task is running, the Mac app looks every few seconds at what's in front: the app's name, its window's title and, from the browser extension, the site and title of the active tab. If you've drifted from the task for a while, it offers to take you back. Reading window titles needs the Accessibility permission, which you grant in macOS settings and can withdraw any time. When the rules can't tell, Apple Intelligence on your Mac decides, if it's available. It never takes screenshots, reads what's inside a page, window or document, or records keystrokes. It keeps only today's tally and the apps and sites you've marked as part of a task. None of it leaves your Mac or syncs. Turn it off in Settings ▸ Guard ▸ Drift guard.
  • Settings, including your tone, your own reminder words, time limits and allowed sites.
  • Turning a brain dump into tasks and judging drift on the Mac use Apple Intelligence on your device when it's available. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else for this.
  • Everything, while sync is off. With sync off, TruFocus sends nothing to our server.

What we receive when sync is on, and why

TruFocus syncs your devices through a server we run on Supabase, in Australia (Sydney). When you turn sync on, your device makes a random key and keeps it in its Keychain. That key never leaves your devices. The server receives:

  • Your tasks, notes and day plans and your focus timeline, end-to-end encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device before upload. The server stores only the encrypted copy. We don't have the key and can't decrypt it.
  • Record details the server needs to sync: a random ID for each record, its type (task, note, session or day), when it last changed, and whether it was deleted. These aren't encrypted, so we could see how many records you have and when they changed, but not what they say.
  • A sync ID: a fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of an access code your device derives from its key. It ties your linked devices together and isn't connected to your name or any account.
  • On iPhone, a push token, so the server can silently wake your phone to sync when your Mac changes something. The wake-up carries no content.
  • When you link a device: a six-digit code, a public key from each device, and your key sealed so only the device you're linking can open it. Both screens show the same four-digit number before anything is sent. This is deleted once the new device has the key, or cleared out if linking doesn't finish.
  • The usual connection details, such as your IP address, which our hosting provider sees on every internet request.

We use this data only to sync your devices. We don't sell it, share it, use it for advertising, or combine it with other data.

Apple services

  • Apple Music. If you choose Apple Music as focus music, TruFocus asks for permission and plays it through Apple's MusicKit, under Apple's privacy policy. We receive nothing.
  • Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device. Silent sync wake-ups go through Apple's push service.

Who processes data for us

Supabase, Inc. hosts the sync database and functions. Apple delivers push notifications. No one else.

How long we keep it

Encrypted records stay on the server until you delete them in Settings ▸ Sync. When you delete a task or note, its encrypted content is removed from the server and a small "deleted" marker remains so your other devices delete it too.

Your choices

  • Leave sync off. TruFocus works fully on one device without it.
  • Turn sync off on a device: Settings ▸ Sync ▸ Turn off here. That device stops sending anything.
  • Delete your data from the server: Settings ▸ Sync ▸ Delete server copy… deletes every synced record and push token for your sync space right away. Do it on each linked device that still has sync on.
  • Don't grant Screen Time access, or revoke it any time in Settings ▸ Screen Time. The task flow still works without it.
  • Turn off the drift guard on the Mac, or remove TruFocus's Accessibility access in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Accessibility.
  • Remove the extension any time from Safari or Chrome settings. Deleting the app removes everything on that device.
  • Access and correction: your data is on your devices in readable form. On the server it's end-to-end encrypted, so we can't read it or hand you anything but ciphertext, and we can't find your sync space from the Sync ID in Settings. Please use Delete server copy… rather than asking us to delete it.

Children

TruFocus isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from them.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Content is end-to-end encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM, under a key that never leaves your devices. The database only answers requests that carry the access code for the matching sync space.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and say what changed in the app's release notes.
